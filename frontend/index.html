Five domains, one clear picture

Pain rarely has one cause. We look at the whole workspace and rank what matters most, instead of handing you a generic list of tips.

  • Ergonomics

    Chair, back support, screen height, keyboard and mouse position.

  • Environment

    Ventilation, light, glare, noise, temperature and air quality.

  • Exposure

    How long you sit at a stretch and how repetitive your hand movement is.

  • Movement

    Break habits and how active you are outside of work.

  • Recovery

    Rest periods, screen breaks and the discomfort you already feel.

    Questions people ask

    Is this a medical tool?

    No. It is a wellness assessment tool. It does not diagnose or predict medical conditions. If you have pain that persists, please see a doctor.

    Do I need a real account?

    No. This is a free demo, so you can register with any email. Nothing is sent to a server.

    Where are my answers stored?

    Only in this browser on your device. Logging out does not delete your assessments, and clearing your browser data does.

    What do I get at the end?

    Findings, exposure and risk indicators, prioritised recommendations, wellness and movement suggestions, and a downloadable PDF report from Sanatan Labs.
